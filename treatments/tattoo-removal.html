What is Tattoo Removal?

Our picosecond laser breaks ink into tiny particles your body clears naturally. Each session fades the tattoo further, whether you want it gone or lightened for a cover-up.


Who it’s for

  • Unwanted tattoos
  • Fading for a cover-up
  • Old or blurred ink
  • Cosmetic tattoo correction
