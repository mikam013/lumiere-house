What is IPL Photofacial?

IPL uses pulses of light to break up sun spots and calm redness. Spots darken for a week and then flake away, revealing more even skin.


Who it’s for

  • Sun spots and freckles
  • Redness and rosacea flushing
  • Broken capillaries
  • Sun-damaged chest and hands

What to expect

  1. Cleanse and eye protection
  2. Light pulses
  3. Cooling and SPF
  4. Spots flake off over 7 days

Pricing

Face$350

Questions

Is it safe for dark skin?

IPL is best for lighter skin types. For deeper tones we’ll suggest a laser or peel instead.
