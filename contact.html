FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer financing?

Yes, 0% for 12 months through Cherry on packages over $500.

Do I need a consultation first?

For lasers, yes: a free 15-minute consult and patch test, which can be the same day as your first session.

Can you treat dark skin?

Yes. Our Nd:YAG laser is designed for deeper skin tones.

What’s the cancellation policy?

24 hours’ notice. Late cancellations count as a package session.

Do packages expire?

Packages are valid for 24 months.

Does it hurt?

A quick snap, like a rubber band. Our lasers have built-in cooling.

Is there downtime?

Usually just a few hours of pinkness.
